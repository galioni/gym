Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026 · Applies to the Daily Grind web app and the Daily Grind mobile apps for iOS and Android.

Daily Grind is a workout tracker. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. We have tried to keep it short and plain. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

2. What we collect

DataWhy we need it
Account: your email address, a password (stored only as a hash by our authentication provider), and, if you use Google or Apple sign-in, the name and email they share with us (Apple may give us a private relay address if you choose to hide your email). To create your account, sign you in, and send you the emails you ask for (confirmation, password reset).

Plan questionnaire answers: goal, experience, days per week, equipment, session length and body-focus areas. To generate a training plan with an AI model (see section 4).

Technical records: our servers log each request (which endpoint, time, status, how long it took, and a one-way hash of your account id, not the id itself), and we count your plan generations and syncs to apply fair-use limits. Our web hosting provider also collects anonymous page-view and performance statistics for the website. To keep the service secure and reliable, to fix faults, and to enforce limits.

Your body weight and workout history can be considered health-related information. You choose whether to enter it, you can delete it at any time, and we use it only to provide the app to you.

The apps do not use advertising identifiers, do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and contain no advertising or third-party analytics. The website uses no advertising cookies.

3. Why we may use your data (legal basis)

  • To provide the service you asked for (account, tracking, sync, plans, subscriptions): performance of our contract with you.
  • Security, fraud prevention, fair-use limits and fixing faults: our legitimate interests in running a safe, reliable service.
  • Health-related entries such as body weight: your consent, given when you choose to enter them. You can withdraw it by deleting the entries or your account.
  • Records we must keep (for example payment records held by Stripe, Apple or Google): legal obligation.

4. Who else handles your data

We use these providers to run Daily Grind. They process data on our behalf, or, for payments, as independent providers.

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. Your account and training data are stored here.
  • Vercel: hosts the website and our server functions, and provides anonymous website analytics and performance measurement.
  • AI model providers: Google (Gemini) by default, and, if you are a Pro subscriber and choose them, Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (ChatGPT). When you generate a plan we send them only the questionnaire answers listed above. We do not send your name, email, account id, workout history, notes or weight. Their own terms apply to what they do with that request.
  • Stripe: card payments for subscriptions bought on the website.
  • Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play): payments for subscriptions bought in the mobile apps. We receive only the purchase identifier and status, never your payment details.
  • Apple also provides Sign in with Apple. When you use it we keep a token from Apple solely so we can tell Apple to revoke it when you delete your account.
  • Resend: sends our account emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset).
  • Google Fonts: the website's dark theme loads its fonts from Google, which means your browser contacts Google's servers.

We may disclose data if the law requires it. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

5. International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards the law provides, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting your data

  • Your account and training data: until you delete your account. In the app and on the website, go to Settings → Data → Delete account and all data. This removes your account, workout history, templates, plans and cloud sync data, deletes your Stripe customer record, and, if you signed in with Apple, revokes our access through your Apple account.
  • Days you delete: their content is erased straight away and a small marker is kept for up to 90 days so the deletion reaches your other devices, then it is removed.
  • Free plan cloud history: the cloud keeps your most recent 7 days; older days stay only on your own device.
  • Technical records: server logs and usage counters are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting, and are not used to build a profile of you.
  • Backups: copies held by our database provider are removed on its normal schedule after deletion.
  • Payment records held by Stripe, Apple and Google follow their own retention rules. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play: cancel it in your store account settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR or EU GDPR) you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and move it to another service, and you can withdraw consent. You can delete your account yourself at any time (section 6). For anything else, email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each person can read and change only their own rows in the database (row-level security), and access to servers and keys is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Children

Daily Grind is not intended for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.
